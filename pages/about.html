ABOUT 3C

She lives in New York. That's all you're getting.

The Girl in 3C is an anonymous collection of field notes about trying to build a beautiful life in a city that has no shortage of beautiful people, bad decisions, and unsolicited opinions.

Dating. Ambition. Friendship. Money. Loneliness. The little things that would sound ridiculous if you said them out loud.

Some stories are hers. Some are yours. Some are a little bit of both.

Names and identifying details may be changed. Some stories may be composites. The point is not perfect journalism. The point is telling the truth about how it felt.

If something happened in New York that you can't stop thinking about, you can tell 3C about it.
