People love to talk about the 'old' New York and how it doesn't exist anymore. While maybe it will never be exactly the same as it once was, it is so much more alive than people give credit for. 

I did a little 'experiment' (I lost my headphones for a week) and the week I went without constantly blocking the city was the best week ever. Genuinely changing not only my view of the city's life but also my philosophy of how to navigate it.

When I say best week ever, it definitely had its up and downs. But that was kind of the best part. This super sweet Brooklyn-native guy yapped with me about the giants for our whole train ride (he saw my shirt), a simple act of helping someone carry down their stroller and then walking away. Now this is the city so there was absolutely the 'bad' as well: the crazy lady screaming slurs and insults at this girl who was sat next to me, the usual zombie shoving his way through the train clicking and clacking as he passed people, the screaming fight that broke out when a girl couldn't squish her way onto the train during rush hour. Normally, I would've drowned it out with my music, but instead I was immersed in it, and something magical happened. The girl who was being verbally attacked, we got off at the same stop and I chatted with her and she ended up crying and hugging me because 'she was fine' but it was so nice to be heard. When the zombie passed through the train, this girl and I made eye contact and shared a little nod. After the screaming match, when the doors finally closed, this older couple squeezed in next to me told be how happy they were that I made it on the train because I deserved to make it. 

In the thick of it, it really was the 'bad' moments that made me feel the most connected to the people of this city, and reminded me that I am not alone and we are all just figuring out our lives together.

Did this 'experiment' convince me to fully give up my headphones? Truthfully most of the time. I genuinely love my home for all it has to offer, the good and the bad. Though the days where I am feeling easily on edge/over stimulated I will always opt for a calming playlist or podcast.

-love, the girl in 3C
