I fear as much as I love men, I love my freedom more. I am young and have a career to work on and girls to go on trips with and so many hobbies and passions and corners of the world to explore. Why would I want to lock myself down with a boyfriend who I have to compromise for? Isn't compromising on skipping a night out to work or passing on a new the cutest new pair of shoes so I can invest enough?

What I do miss though- the *drama.* There is just something about the game of dating that really makes a girl feel alive. Especially if it's a situationship. gasp. I know, I know how bad of me. But is my early 20's really the time for me to meet my husband when I haven't even met my maid of honor or bridesmaids yet? Exactly, but as fun as being 'locked in' is and how oh so very lifemaxxing it is. Sometimes a girl just needs a little drama. A good situationship is the perfect way to introduce that drama without real strings or having to believe that man is the one for you. Does that make me the villain? Maybe to some, but if men can be man whores in their 'prime' then I think us girlies can have a situationship here and there.

You either get it or you don't. 

- love, the girl in 3C
