I fear as much as I love men, I love my freedom more.

I’m young. I have a career to work on, girls to go on trips with, hobbies and passions, and so many corners of the world left to explore.

Why would I want to lock myself down with a boyfriend who I have to constantly compromise for?

And isn’t that what relationships are?

Compromise?

Skipping a night out because you have work in the morning. Staying in when you’d rather go out. Passing on the cutest new pair of shoes because you’re trying to save enough to invest in something else.

And I’m sorry, but I’m not ready for that.

At least not yet.

What I *do* miss, though?

The drama.

There is just something about the game of dating that makes a girl feel alive.

The anticipation.

The flirting.

The wondering if he’s going to text.

The “what does he mean by that?”

The getting dressed for a date and wondering what the night is going to turn into.

Especially when it’s a situationship.

GASP.

I know. I know.

How very toxic of me.

But is my early twenties really the time for me to meet my husband when I haven’t even met my maid of honor or bridesmaids yet?

Exactly.

I have places to go.

People to meet.

Mistakes to make.

And an entire life to build before I decide who I want standing next to me for the rest of it.

As fun as being “locked in” is (and as oh-so-very lifemaxxing of me), sometimes a girl just needs a little drama.

A good situationship is the perfect way to introduce that drama without the pressure of having to decide that this man is the one.

You get the butterflies.

The flirting.

The excitement.

The occasional emotional damage.

And then you go home to your own apartment.

What more could a girl want?

Does that make me the villain?

Maybe.

But if men can be man-whores in their “prime,” I think us girlies can have a situationship here and there.

I’m not saying I never want a relationship.

I do.

Eventually.

I want the great love story.

I want someone who makes me want to cancel my plans.

I just don’t want to spend my twenties trying to find him when there is so much life left to live in the meantime.

So for now?

I’ll take the freedom.

I’ll take the flirting.

I’ll take the occasional questionable decision.

And, if the universe is feeling generous, I’ll take a man who ruins my life a *little.*

You either get it or you don’t.

-love, the girl in 3C
