Let me just start by saying I am so sorry if you have ever been this girl.

Though, let’s be honest, we pretty much all have.

I have been told I’m too picky, or shallow, or whatnot. But honestly, I just have a type. And I have made the mistake of dating someone who didn’t fit that type before.

Nowadays, I know better.

And I’m not afraid to stand up for that.

Now for the story.

I’m sure most everyone can relate. You get asked out on a date by this guy. He’s jacked, sweet, and very down to earth. All in all, a great guy and a super date.

Just one itty bitty problem.

This man has the ugliest face ever.

But he really is so sweetie and such a great guy, and he’s totally your type otherwise. So you think, *Okay, maybe this is what being mature looks like. Maybe I’m being shallow. Maybe attraction will grow.*

So you start dating.

He asks you to be his girlfriend. You get close. You spend all your time in each other’s apartments. You meet each other’s families and friends. You say you love each other. You talk about the future.

Everything is great.

Except…

In bed, you close your eyes so you don’t see his face.

It’s tough to look him in the eyes when you’re saying “I love you.”

You start noticing that you’re avoiding pictures together.

You don’t really want to look at him for too long.

And slowly, almost without realizing it, you start feeling yourself shutting down to him.

Until one day you have a big fight.

And suddenly you realize something horrible:

You’ve been waiting for this exact moment.

Not because the fight was actually the reason you wanted to leave, but because you finally have a reason to.

You tell yourself you’re breaking up because of the fight.

But deep down, you know the relationship was never going to last.

Because you were never fully attracted to him in the first place.

Now he’s hurt, you’re hurt, and you spent months and months in a relationship with someone you knew, somewhere deep down, you didn’t actually want.

And honestly?

That’s not fair to either of you.

I genuinely believe that if you know, you know.

That’s not to say you can’t compromise. Or that attraction can’t grow. Or that a guy couldn’t seem perfect and then hit you with the biggest red flag you’ve ever seen a week, a month, or even a couple months into dating.

And, as my unfortunate friend found out the hard way, even ugly guys can be jerks and cheat on you.

So what’s the point?

I’m not saying you need to date a model.

I’m saying you should actually *want* to date the person you’re dating.

Because “he’s such a nice guy” is not the same thing as chemistry.

And “but he checks every box” doesn’t mean much if you don’t want to kiss him.

I think women are taught that having standards makes us shallow. That we should give the nice guy a chance. That attraction will come later. That maybe we’re being too picky.

And sometimes, sure, maybe we are.

But sometimes you just know.

And I think there is something to be said for trusting yourself before you get six months into a relationship and start praying for a fight dramatic enough to give you an excuse to leave.

So yes.

I stopped giving ugly guys a chance.

Not because I think ugly men don’t deserve love.

They absolutely do.

Just… maybe not from me.

And in a city of 8 million people, why would I pick an ugly one?

-love, the girl in 3C
