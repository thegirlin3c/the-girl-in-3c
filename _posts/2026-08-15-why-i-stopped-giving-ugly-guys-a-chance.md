Let me just start by saying I am so sorry if you have ever been this girl- though we pretty much all have. 

I have been told I'm too picky, or shallow, or whatnot. But honestly, I just have a type and have made the mistake of dating someone who didn't fit that already. Nowadays I know better and am not afraid to stand up for that.

Now for the story. I'm sure most everyone can related, you get asked out on a date by this guy. He's jacked, sweet, and is very down to earth. All in all a great guy and a super date. Just one itty bitty problem. This man has the ugliest face ever. *But* he really is so sweetie and a great guy and totally your type otherwise. So you start dating, he asks you to be his girlfriend, you get close, spend all your time in each other's apartments, meet the other's families and friends. You say you love each other, talk about a future, everything is so *great.* Except in bed, ya know, you close your eyes so you don't see his face, and it's tough to look at him in the eyes when your saying I love you, and slowly you start feeling yourself shutting down to him. Until one day you have a big fight, and you realize you have been waiting for this exact moment so you can have a reason to break up. You tell yourself you're breaking up because of the fight but you know deep down the relationship was never going to last. Now he's hurt and you spent months and months being in a relationship with someone you never were fully attracted to from the start. 

I genuinely believe that if you know, you know. That's not to say you can't compromise or that a guy could seem perfect and then hit you with the biggest red flag a week or two or even a couple months into dating. Or as my unfortunate friend found out the hard way, even ugly guys can be jerks and cheat on you. So in a city of 8 million, why would you pick an ugly one?

-love, the girl in 3C
