I don't care how much money you spent on them, that doesn't stop them from being ugly. I am sure this will make a lot of the girlies mad but that's what a hot take is so you're gonna have to live with it.

P.S. reading this is in fact optional

Now to the matter at hand. Ballet flats: so cute, so chic, so perf. Why in the world would we see something so cute and decide, hey let's take something everyone tries to avoid in their pants (the dreaded camel toe) and let's put it on our feet. Genuinely felt like a punking.

I swear when I first saw them in a magazine, I remember saying to my friend > "I pray this doesn't become a new trend." Sure enough a couple weeks later my friend sent me the first camel toe sighting, and it wasn't long after that, that I saw them everywhere. 

Not only do they look uggo, but they also are not comfy. Like ballet flats are such a perfect go to staple every girl should have. They are wearable all day but still very classy. These b-list flat downgrades feel like having a thick hair stuck in between you toes. Personally, if I am going to have sore or painful shoes on it's because they are the highest, sexiest stilettos this side of the tri-state area.

No this isn't an ad for ballet flats, I just really love my little flats. Which I will be wearing long after this little trend is over.

-love, the girl in 3C
