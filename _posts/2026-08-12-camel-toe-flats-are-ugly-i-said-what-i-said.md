I don’t care how much money you spent on them.

That doesn’t stop them from being ugly.

I am sure this will make a lot of the girlies mad, but that’s what a hot take is, so you’re just going to have to live with it.

P.S. Reading this is, in fact, optional.

Now, to the matter at hand.

Ballet flats:

So cute.

So chic.

So perf.

Why in the world would we look at something so cute and decide: *Hey, let’s take something everyone spends their entire lives trying to avoid in their pants, the dreaded camel toe, and put it on our feet.*

Genuinely felt like a punking.

I swear, when I first saw them in a magazine, I remember saying to my friend:

“I pray this doesn’t become a new trend.”

Sure enough, a couple weeks later, my friend sent me the first camel-toe sighting.

And it wasn’t long after that I started seeing them everywhere.

Everywhere.

Not only do they look uggo, but they’re not even comfortable.

And this is where I really don’t understand the trend.

Ballet flats are such a perfect go-to staple. Every girl should have a pair. They’re wearable all day, they’re easy to throw on, and they still look chic.

They’re basically the perfect shoe.

So why did we decide to take them and make them look like…

That?

These B-list flat downgrades feel like having a thick hair stuck between your toes all day.

And personally, if I’m going to have sore or painful feet at the end of the day, it’s going to be because I’m wearing the highest, sexiest stilettos this side of the Tri-State Area.

Not because my shoes look like they’re trying to recreate a wedgie.

No, this is not an ad for ballet flats.

I just really love my little flats.

Which I will be wearing long after this little trend is over.

I’ll be over here.

In my normal ballet flats.

Looking chic.

And camel-toe free.

-love, the girl in 3C
