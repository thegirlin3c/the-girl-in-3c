The unfortunate reality of dating today.

Your friends are always telling you that you should start dating and put yourself out there. And they say it like you wouldn’t love to be dating. Like you wouldn’t adore cute dates, getting to know someone, having someone to go places with, and having that whole thing.

But it’s not that you’re not open to it. It’s that men no longer seem to approach women in public. Meet-cutes are few and far between. So, naturally, everyone tells you to put yourself out there, and eventually you resort to… the dreaded dating apps.

Surely that will help your dating life.

Or, you know, your lack thereof. Lol.

Except the guys on there are either ugly, looking to hook up, or so full of themselves that they seem to think they’re entitled to a hookup without even attempting to get to know you first. God forbid they pretend to like you for a few dates before making a move.

Recently, I had the absolute pleasure of matching with a *True Man.*

HA.

What actually happened was this: he liked my profile, which, if he was literate, he would have noticed very clearly stated that my dating goals were not casual or even short-term. He was pretty cute (remember, we stopped giving ugly guys a chance longgg ago), so you decide to match.

Almost immediately, he asks what you’re looking for.

Okay. He’s asking. That’s promising, I guess?

So you explain.

Then he asks if that means you’re waiting until marriage.

I KNOW. I KNOW.

Red flag. Should’ve immediately unmatched.

But sometimes I, too, am weak. Sometimes I want to believe there are still quality dating prospects out there.

So you answer his question.

He says, “Oh, so serious relationships then.”

Yes. That is the goal.

But you’re also a very busy person with a lot going on, and sometimes you just want a situationship. And if it’s exclusive? You could work with that. You’re not looking to get married tomorrow. You’re simply looking for something that isn’t completely devoid of intention.

So again, you explain.

He then jumps to “risking getting sick” because you’re currently fighting a cold, which you had already explained meant you weren’t free.

So you shut that down and make sure to clarify that you don’t hook up right off the bat.

His response?

You have a dirty mind.

Which, yes, maybe I do. But in this particular instance, it was pretty clear where his mind was.

You explain that no, you just don’t trust men.

Who does these days?

Then you ask what he’s looking for, because he never actually answered his own question. Even though his profile says “relationship.”

As you’re sending that, he responds to your lack of faith in men by calling you a lesbian.

Babes, I wishhhh.

I genuinely wish I wasn’t straight, especially in moments like these.

Then he says he’s looking for your Instagram.

AS IF.

So you give him a little attitude and, to cut a long story short, he tells you he doesn’t chase.

He attracts.

Sir.

That’s just not how a real man should function.

I’m not saying a man shouldn’t have standards. Of course he should. But if you’re interested in a woman, you should probably make an effort to show her that you’re interested in her.

Especially if she’s already built a life she likes.

Because here’s the other thing: men loooove to say they want a “high value woman.”

You know the one.

She has her own place. She’s hardworking. She has dreams and aspirations. She has hobbies and a personality. She works out. She’s good looking. She’s elegant. She takes care of herself. She’s independent. She’s interesting.

Basically, she can do everything and provide almost everything for herself.

Honey, that woman doesn’t need a man.

So what exactly are you offering her?

Because if she’s already built a life she loves, the thing that makes you worth adding to it is how you treat her.

And the best way to show that is to actually make an effort.

Approach her.

Plan the date.

Court her.

Try to impress her.

Make her feel wanted.

I’m not saying women should never pursue men. If you want to, by all means, go for it.

But personally, I have no interest in chasing a man who has decided that being mildly attractive and having an Instagram account is enough to make me chase him.

And I certainly don’t think a woman should have to bend over backwards to “score” a man who isn’t even giving her the bare minimum.

Which brings me back to my original point.

Where are the gentlemen?

Because I’m starting to fear they’ve gone extinct.

Maybe there’s one left somewhere in New York City. Maybe he’s currently holding a door open for someone, planning an actual date, and making a woman feel like the most beautiful person in the room.

If he is, I hope the universe knows I’m looking.

In the meantime, I’ll continue my Dua Lipa-inspired manifesting techniques.

At this point, what’s there to lose?

-love, the girl in 3C
