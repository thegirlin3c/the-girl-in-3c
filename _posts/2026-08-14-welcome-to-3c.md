There are approximately eight million people in New York, and somehow it can still feel like everyone knows everyone.

I live here.

That's all you're getting.

Names will be changed. Details may be rearranged. Some stories are composites. The important parts are the feelings, the ridiculousness, and whatever lesson I was apparently supposed to learn the first time.

This is a place for dating stories, city observations, ambition, friendship, money, beautiful mistakes, terrible decisions, and the occasional thing that is too funny not to write down.

For now, the stories are mine.

Eventually, I want to hear yours.

Welcome to 3C.
